# Consumer and community strategy review

1. Review the 15-county geography table, data vintage, and geocode exceptions. These are locations and roster counts, not patient demand.
2. Review unique school catchments, the 600 overlap-pair correction, and radius sensitivity. Do not add overlapping venue counts to estimate unique families.
3. Collect authorized service-line demand, available appointment capacity, contribution margin, referral access, and brand priorities. Define acquisition, retention, loyalty, and access KPIs and owners.
4. Request comparable sports partnership proposals with inventory, dates, exclusivity, attendance methodology, community-school access, activation rights, fees, and measurable deliverables. Evaluate professional sports, school, and league proposals on the same disclosed criteria. Public facility geography alone cannot price a sponsorship.
5. Create the annual marketing plan, channel brief, content calendar, budget, and quarterly reporting schedule. Assign agency ownership, creative approvals, provider biography review, clinical review, and brand asset sign-off.
6. Run the companion `brand_consumer_readiness` module before campaign launch. Confirm capacity and referral readiness. Hold or pivot campaigns if expected responses exceed operational access.
7. Use the mystery shopping template for authorized observations across search, website, phone, referral, scheduling, and follow-up. Record elapsed time, successful next step, issue owner, remediation, and recheck date.
8. Review spend, qualified inquiries, scheduled and completed visits, retention cohort eligibility, and incremental contribution quarterly. Keep historical source outcomes, operating actuals, and planning scenarios separate.
